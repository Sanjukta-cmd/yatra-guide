Aviation & Airport Connectivity

Vrindavan does not have its own commercial airport. Best entry options below:

Primary International Hub:

IGI Airport, New Delhi (DEL)

160 km from Vrindavan. Take the Delhi Airport Express / Southern Peripheral Road directly onto Yamuna Expressway via cab (~3.5 hrs).

Nearest Domestic Airport:

Agra Kheria Airport (AGR) / Jewar Airport (DXN)

Agra Airport is 70 km south (1.5h drive). Jewar International Airport (opening on the expressway) will be just 75 km away.

Monkey & Spectacle Safety

Vrindavan and Banke Bihari lanes are famous for swift monkeys. Keep eyeglasses, sunglasses, and smartphones inside zipped bags before entering temple corridors.

🛺

Local E-Rickshaw Navigation

Outside cars are restricted near inner temples on weekends. Park your car at designated expressway lots (e.g. VIP parking or Chhatikara) and use local shared E-rickshaws (₹20-₹40 per person).

🥛

Must-Try Street Delicacies

Mathura Peda at Brijwasi, hot morning Bedmi Puri with spicy aloo sabzi, and creamy Kulhad Rabdi & Lassi near Loi Bazaar.
